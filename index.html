HOW TO PLAY

Step behind the bar. Reconstruct the authentic, canonical recipe specification card for the day's featured cocktail.

1
Study the Order Ticket: Read the cocktail name, era, and historical balance clues.
2
Fill the 5 Blueprint Slots: Select components from the Bar Rail to assign:
  • Base Spirit & Measure
  • Modifier / Liqueur & Measure
  • Balance / Citrus / Accent
  • Technique & Ice Preparation
  • Glassware & Garnish
3
Test Your Spec: You have 4 Shakers (attempts).
  • Correct specs lock with gold trim.
  • Incorrect specs are ejected back to the bar rail.
4
Master the Curriculum: A new cocktail unlocks every midnight. Past releases enter the permanent Vault Archive.
